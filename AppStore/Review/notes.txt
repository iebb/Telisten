Telisten is a focused music player for audio available in the user's Telegram account. It connects directly from the device to Telegram with MTProto and does not use TDLib, a bot, a proxy, or a developer-operated backend.

Telisten does not expose a general messaging interface. It extracts compatible audio documents for playback and ignores text, photos, video, and other message types. It does not send Telegram read-history calls, so browsing or playing music does not mark conversations as read.

Use the Telegram account supplied in App Review Sign-In Information. Telisten supports codes delivered by Telegram or email and Telegram two-step verification. After login, the library intentionally opens without selecting a source.

Review path:
1. Choose Search All Music and search for a track.
2. Select an audio result. The player appears immediately while the first bytes stream.
3. Open the compact player to see playback controls, synchronized lyrics, and Up Next.
4. Save the track to a playlist. Telisten creates or reuses private Telegram storage and forwards the original audio document.
5. Open Downloads to confirm the cached track and cache usage.

External services:
• Telegram data centers: account authentication, music search, audio streaming and downloads, and private playlist storage.
• lrclib.net: lyrics matching using track title, artist, and duration.
