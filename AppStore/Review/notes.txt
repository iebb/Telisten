Telisten is a focused Telegram user client for music. It connects directly from the device to Telegram with MTProto and does not use TDLib, a bot, a proxy, or a developer-operated backend.

Use the Telegram account supplied in App Review Sign-In Information. Telisten supports codes delivered by Telegram or email and Telegram two-step verification. After login, the library intentionally opens with no chat selected.

Review path:
1. Choose a music chat or Search All Music.
2. Tap an audio row. The player appears immediately while the first bytes stream.
3. Tap the compact player to open Now Playing and view timed lyrics, comments, and Up Next.
4. Add to playlist. Telisten creates or reuses a private Telegram channel in the _Playlist folder and forwards the original audio message.
5. A vote is a Telegram thumbs-up reaction. A comment is a Telegram reply.

The app does not mark messages or chats as read. It reads message history/search results and fetches audio without sending Telegram read-history calls.

External services:
• Telegram data centers: account authentication, chats, messages, media, reactions, replies, and playlist channels.
• lrclib.net: lyrics matching using track title, artist, and duration.

The Debug-only --demo launch argument is not available in the submitted Release build.
