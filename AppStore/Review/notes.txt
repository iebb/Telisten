Telisten is a focused music player for audio available in the user's Telegram account. It connects directly from the device to Telegram with MTProto and does not use TDLib, a bot, a proxy, or a developer-operated backend.

Telisten does not expose a general messaging interface. It extracts compatible audio documents for playback and ignores text, photos, video, and other message types. It does not send Telegram read-history calls, so browsing or playing music does not mark conversations as read.

Use the Telegram account supplied in App Review Sign-In Information. Telisten supports codes delivered by Telegram or email and Telegram two-step verification. After login, the library intentionally opens without selecting a source.

Review path:
1. Choose Search All Music and search for a track.
2. Select an audio result. The player appears immediately while the first bytes stream.
3. Open the compact player to see playback controls, synchronized lyrics, and Up Next.
4. Save the track to a playlist. Telisten creates or reuses private Telegram storage and forwards the original audio document.
5. Open Downloads to confirm the cached track and cache usage.

External services:
• Telegram data centers: account authentication, music search, audio streaming and downloads, and private playlist storage.
• lrclib.net: lyrics matching using track title, artist, and duration.

The Debug-only --demo launch argument is not available in the submitted Release build.

Guideline 4.3 clarification:
We respectfully disagree that Telisten is spam or a duplicate/template app. Telisten is one independently designed native music product, not a reskin, generic Telegram wrapper, or collection of similar apps. Its product and implementation are specific to turning audio already accessible in a user's Telegram account into a focused music library.

Build 23 demonstrates the app's distinctive functionality:
• A custom direct MTProto client, without TDLib, a bot, a proxy, or a developer-operated media backend.
• Global, deduplicated music search and lazy music-only indexing that does not mark chats as read.
• Progressive playback before a download completes, plus a shared offline cache that reuses full and partial downloads across Telegram accounts.
• Telegram-backed playlists that preserve the original audio by forwarding it to dedicated private storage, with a local mirror for ordering and offline launch.
• Synchronized LRCLIB lyrics, multiple-match selection, album artwork, background audio, and native iOS and macOS interfaces.
• Listen Together through a Telegram group audio chat. Only groups or channels administered by the signed-in user can host a session. The host can share an invite link or invite Telegram contacts; participants can hear the live audio chat with Telegram, while Telisten clients also follow the current track and position.

These are substantive, integrated music-player workflows rather than duplicated content or minimal variations on another app. Please reconsider the Guideline 4.3 classification. If App Review believes Telisten duplicates a specific app or submission, please identify it so we can address that concern directly.
