Telisten is a native player for the music already in your Telegram account. Sign in, choose any chat with audio, and start listening without copying links or sending media through another service.

STREAM AND SEARCH
• Start playback while a track is still loading
• Seek, skip, shuffle, play in order or reverse order, and repeat one
• Search one chat or search music across all accessible chats
• Keep search results tidy with automatic duplicate removal

KEEP MUSIC CLOSE
• Download tracks for offline listening
• Keep a bounded 2 GB local cache with simple download controls
• See embedded album artwork and Telegram chat avatars when available
• Use background playback and system media controls

TELEGRAM-NATIVE PLAYLISTS
• Keep playlists as private Telegram channels in a _Playlist folder
• Save a track by forwarding its original Telegram message
• Rename, reorder, and remove playlist entries in Telisten

LYRICS AND CONVERSATION
• Follow synchronized lyrics matched through LRCLIB
• See the current and next lyric lines in the compact player
• Vote with Telegram reactions
• Read and add comments as Telegram replies

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Telisten connects directly to Telegram with MTProto. It does not use TDLib, a bot, a proxy service, or a Kitta Co backend. Telegram authorization keys stay in the system Keychain, and downloaded media stays on your device.

Telisten is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Telegram or LRCLIB. Availability of media, forwarding, comments, and lyrics depends on the source chat and service.
