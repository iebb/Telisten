Telisten is a native player for the music already available in your Telegram account. Sign in, find a track, and start listening without copying links or sending audio through another service.

PLAY AND DISCOVER
• Start playback while a track is still loading
• Seek, skip, shuffle, play in order or reverse order, and repeat one
• Search your accessible music collection with automatic duplicate removal
• See only supported audio; text, photos, and other message types are ignored

KEEP MUSIC CLOSE
• Download tracks for offline listening
• Choose a cache limit from 100 MB to 50 GB
• Reuse cached and partially downloaded audio instead of downloading it twice
• See embedded album artwork when available
• Continue listening in the background with system media controls

BUILD PLAYLISTS
• Create private, cloud-backed playlists
• Save the original audio without making another copy
• Rename playlists and reorder or remove tracks locally

FOLLOW THE LYRICS
• Match lyrics through LRCLIB using title and duration
• Prefer synchronized lyrics when several matches are available
• Choose a different match whenever you want
• Follow timed lines in the compact and expanded players

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Telisten connects directly to Telegram with MTProto. It does not use TDLib, a bot, a proxy service, or a Kitta Ltd backend. Telegram authorization keys stay in the system Keychain, and downloaded media stays on your device.

Telisten is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Telegram or LRCLIB. Music and lyrics availability depends on the source and service.
